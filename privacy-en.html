Privacy Policy

Heavy Machinery Empire (Império das Máquinas) · imperiodasmaquinaspesadas.com.br · Updated October 2026 · Português

1. What the game stores on your device

Your progress (account, cash, machines, contracts and headquarters) is saved on your own phone or browser. This data is not sent to us. If you clear your browser data, your progress may be lost, which is why the game lets you generate a backup code.

2. Anonymous usage data (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to understand how the game is used and to improve it. It may collect, anonymously: screens viewed, time spent, device and browser type, approximate location (city or region) and in-game events (for example, game start, contract accepted, HQ purchased). IP addresses are anonymized. We do not collect your name, government ID, email or phone number through Analytics.

Google Analytics may use cookies or browser storage to recognize your device between visits.

How to opt out: you can block cookies in your browser or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

3. Payment

The optional one-time game unlock is processed by Mercado Pago, in Brazilian reais (BRL). Card and other payment details are handled directly by Mercado Pago under its own policy. The game only stores the confirmation that the payment was approved.

4. Ads

The game may show third-party ads in the future. If that happens, this policy will be updated.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Anonymous usage data stays with Google Analytics, which acts as a processor of that data.

6. Your rights

You may ask us about the data we hold about you, request correction or deletion, or ask us to stop collecting it, and you may decline usage-data collection at any time (see section 2). Brazilian users are covered by the LGPD (Law 13,709/2018); U.S. residents may exercise the rights granted by their state's privacy laws.

7. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us data, please contact us.

8. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: Imperiodasmaquinaspesadas@gmail.com
